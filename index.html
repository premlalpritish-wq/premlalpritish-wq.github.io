01 — Flagship Project Spotlight

Mechanistic-ML Bioreactor Digital Twin

GAM-86 · Serial Grey-Box Architecture Interactive Monograph

Hybrid Mechanistic-ML Fed-Batch Bioreactor Digital Twin (GAM-86)

An interactive scientific monograph and real-time simulation workbench coupling mass-conservation ODEs with an 86-parameter serial grey-box architecture. Uses a positivity-preserving Patankar–Rosenbrock stiff solver and Continuous-Discrete Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) soft-sensor state estimation to evaluate scale-up dynamics from 5 L benchtop reactors to 1,000 L production vessels.
